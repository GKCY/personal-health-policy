Personal Health Tracker

A private workflow for understanding personal sleep, activity, and recovery records.

This site publishes the policies for a personal project under development. The intended workflow includes local records and analysis, with optional AI-assisted analysis chosen by the owner where the data source permits it. The project is for GKCY's own use and is not a service open to other users.
